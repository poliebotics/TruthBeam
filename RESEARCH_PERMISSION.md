## Draft grant

Subject to the draft status above, and only when owner/counsel approval and publication as effective
have occurred, **Cathal Ryan Hynes grants** a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free permission to use
those Materials solely for **non-commercial research, evaluation, and education**, including
permission to:

- download and keep local research copies;
- inspect, analyse, recompute, and evaluate the Materials;
- train and evaluate research models using the Materials;
- publish results, metrics, papers, and the minimum non-identifiable illustrative excerpts reasonably
  needed to explain those results;
- make local modifications to source, documentation, and data-processing materials for those purposes;
  and
- build or adapt projector-camera rigs for research using published source and documentation, insofar as
  the activity requires only copyright or database rights controlled by the grantor and is directed to
  reproducing, verifying, or comparing the published measurements.

Cross-witness trials, protocol extensions, and new PolieBotics applications are welcomed through a
separately agreed collaboration with Cathal Ryan Hynes and the project. They are not included merely
because a recipient downloaded the data or built a rig.

This proposed permission does not convert the repository into open-source software and does not permit
commercial deployment or redistribution of the Materials as a substitute dataset or product.

## Conditions

Any permitted publication or shared derived work must:

1. attribute **Cathal Ryan Hynes / PolieBotics Truth Beam**, identify the repository or release used, and
   cite the whitepaper and relevant patent publication where appropriate;
2. state material modifications and avoid implying that the grantor endorses the modified work or its
   conclusions;
3. preserve notices and report results with the release's declared measurement scope; and
4. comply with applicable law, institutional ethics requirements, and any third-party rights.

## Identifiable captures and excluded uses

The proposed permission does **not** allow redistribution of raw identifiable captures, or of derived
media that preserves or enables reconstruction of the performer's identifiable likeness. Aggregate
metrics, non-identifiable transforms, and result publications remain within the proposed research scope
when they cannot reasonably be used to recover that material.

The Materials may not be used for:

- biometric identification or re-identification;
- surveillance of a person or group;
- inference of sensitive traits; or
- a commercial product, paid service, operational deployment, or other commercial advantage without a
  separate written agreement.
